Steven D. Shaw & Gideon Nave (Wharton School, UPenn): Identified as the primary researchers behind the concept of "System 3" as artificial cognition. They introduced the, "Thinking—Fast, Slow, and Artificial" framework, which explores how AI acts as an augmented, often external, cognitive layer that interacts with human intuition (System 1) and deliberation (System 2).Michael Anton Dila: A notable writer and thinker who has been exploring and publishing on "System 3: Initial Hypotheses" since 2022, often defining it in terms of meta-cognition or advanced, integrative thinking.Olli Tikkanen (CEO): Has written on the concept of "System 3" as a hybrid, emergent cognitive layer formed by continuous interaction between human judgment and AI.Peter Webb & Planet Lean: Have explored "System 3" in earlier contexts, focusing on a more "considerative" or emotionally resonant, deliberate way of assessment (sometimes referred to as the "reflective" or "imaginative" system).
