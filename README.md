# Protocols

Working repository for [Robert Peake](https://www.robertpeake.com)'s research and projects on the protocols of human-AI collaboration. Four threads.

## The Prufrock Protocol

A protocol for marking time through cryptographically-signed responses to random interruptions, framed by poetic form. Pitch in development for [The Long Now Foundation](https://longnow.org/) Lab 001.1 — *Book of Time* — in conversation with the [Protocols Institute](https://summerofprotocols.com/protocols-institute). Submission: 2026-06-05.

Anchor texts: `prufrock/pitch.md`, `prufrock/formalisation.md`, `prufrock/protocol-diagram.svg`.

## Effective Protocols for Human-AI Collaboration

The broader research thread under which Prufrock sits. Asks how human collaboration paradigms (GTD, agile, pair programming; older traditions of ritual and ceremony) might be transposed into transhuman protocols that span the human-AI boundary, and how to measure their effectiveness against not just task output but cognitive load, sovereignty, governance, and social impact.

Anchor: `transhuman-protocols/STATEMENT.md`. The thread also holds a daily research-surveillance pipeline, distillations of the *Protocol Reader* and the Formal Protocol Theory SIG corpus, and adjacent reference material.

## Poetry as Protocol

Archive of the July 2025 talk, *Poetry as Protocol*, given at the Edge Esmeralda Protocol School and reprised at the Protocols Institute. The talk formalises poetic practice as a recursive rule-learning algorithm and was the conversation that opened the door to the Prufrock work.

Slides, handouts, and notes: `poetry-talk/`.
