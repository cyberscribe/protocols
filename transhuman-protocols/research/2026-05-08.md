- **Protocol mechanisms are commodifying** — Routines (B3), Outcomes/rubrics (B2/B5), Dreams (B4), multi-agent orchestration (A2/B5/B6) all landed as product primitives in a single keynote. Worth a framework note: protocol-as-platform-feature changes the design question from "what protocol works" to "what protocol does the platform make defaultable." Treat as a candidate addition rather than a confirmed move — two practitioner observations isn't enough yet.
