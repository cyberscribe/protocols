## Method

Names scored against the five criteria in `mission.md` (positive valence · points at method not machine ·
carries both surfaces · survives all audiences · avoids the generic), then filtered through three
tradeoff axes:

- **Authentic** — does the word actually *mean* what the corpus means (trust-relocation, the learning
  loop, sovereignty, growth-through-struggle), or is it decoration?
- **Principled** — does it hold up under the dignity stance (`knowing-was-never-the-point.md`) and avoid
  misrepresenting us to any audience, including the encyclical-informed skeptic?
- **Effective** — memorable, ownable, low market crowding, viable domain/trademark lane.

A name that only scores on one or two axes is still listed; the gap is named rather than hidden.

## The finding that reordered the list

My two strongest *conceptual* candidates — **Keel** (trust the structural thing that lets you harness a
powerful force) and **Metis** (Greek: situated practical wisdom vs. codified *techne* — almost literally
our thesis) — are both **already crowded in the exact AI lane**, badly enough to demote them:

- **Keel** — Keel Ventures (AI incubator), Keel3 ("AI transformation partner"), Say Keel (agentic AI
  studio), Keel Consulting (AI/RPA). The metaphor is ours; the lane is taken.
- **Metis** — `withmetis.ai` is a funded 2025 YC-adjacent startup selling "the missing learning and
  evaluation layer so agentic computing becomes dependable" — that is our sentence. Plus an AI edge chip
  (Axelera), a defunct data-science bootcamp, and an L2 chain. Direct, unwinnable collision.

Honest consequence: the best etymology and the best metaphor are not the best *available* names. The
survivors below trade a little conceptual perfection for an ownable lane.

## The two I'd actually build on

### Whetstone — the venture/institute brand

The whetstone is the cleanest object-metaphor for the mission's third commitment, *growth over shortcut*:
a stone that does nothing on its own, adds no material to the blade, yet the blade leaves sharper — and
the edge belongs to the blade, not the stone. That is the gym-buddy/spotter frame and the sovereignty
qualifier in a single image: **the human is the thing that gets better; the tool is instrumental and
subordinate.** It reads as partnership-with-growth, not human-replacement — exactly the register the
encyclical problem demands.

- **Authentic:** high on growth/sovereignty; lighter on "protocol/trust-relocation" (it encodes the
  outcome of practice, not the machinery). Acceptable — the machinery can live one layer down (see Kata).
- **Principled:** dignity-aligned, positive, survives enterprise / researcher / public / skeptic.
- **Effective:** distinctive and ownable as a mark; low in-lane crowding; **domain is the one real cost**
  — the clean `.com` is gone, so you'd anchor on `whetstone.institute` (research) + a modifier `.com`, or
  buy the exact `.com` off its current holder. Both surfaces read well: *Whetstone Institute* (library),
  *Whetstone* (practice/consulting).

### Kata — the unit of practice in the library

Independent of the brand decision, **kata is the right word for the core object of the research library.**
A protocol, in the corpus, is *frozen learning* — a form extracted from repeated experiment and drilled
until it reliably turns intent into outcome. That is the definition of a kata. And Toyota Kata (Rother)
already gives you a credentialed lineage the README's systems/lean/agile audience will recognize on
sight: the *improvement kata* (iterate scientifically toward a target) and the *coaching kata* (procedural
guidance, not answers) are your learning loop and your human-holds-direction principle, pre-named.

- **Authentic:** the single closest word to both the mission spine ("trust your *practice*") and the
  research mechanism (protocol = drilled form).
- **Effective:** hard to *own* as a company name (generic, un-trademarkable bare), which is exactly why it
  works better as a **term-of-art** than a masthead. "A Whetstone kata" / "the kata library" reads
  naturally.

**The resolution this suggests for `mission.md`'s open question** (does the research thread rename with the
business?): you may not have to choose one word for both arms. **Whetstone** as the brand across both
surfaces; **kata** as the internal unit the library is built from. One name sells and protects; the other
does the conceptual work. If you insist on a single word for everything, Whetstone is the more defensible
unifier — *Kata Institute* is generic, *Whetstone Institute* is not.

### Cairn — hold as dark horse

Worth keeping in play for one reason the other two miss: it's the only candidate that natively encodes the
**knowledge-commons** strand from `knowing-was-never-the-point.md` and the collapse corpus. A cairn is
built by one traveller as a byproduct of making the passage, and it guides the next — the private struggle
whose residue feeds the shared base. If the venture's public story leads with *collective* knowledge
health rather than *individual* capability, Cairn becomes the most authentic pick. It's warmer and more
distinctive than most institute names, low AI-crowding, `cairn.institute` open. The gap is Effective: `.com`/`.ai`
gone, and "cairn" doesn't self-explain "method" the way whetstone/kata do — it needs a tagline to land.

## Recommendation

Lead with **Whetstone** for the brand (both arms), and adopt **kata** as the library's term-of-art for a
codified protocol. Keep **Cairn** as the live alternative *if* you decide the commons angle, not the
individual-capability angle, is the front door. Treat **Keel** and **Metis** as retired — right ideas,
wrong century of availability.

Immediate check before committing to Whetstone: pull an authoritative WHOIS + a USPTO/□ knock-out search
on "Whetstone" in the training/education/consulting classes (the ed-tech user vacated the name but may
still hold a live mark), and price the exact-match `.com`.

## Open

- Does the front-door story lead with *individual capability* (→ Whetstone) or *collective knowledge

- Tagline pairing — "trust your practice, not the model" survives under any of these and does the
