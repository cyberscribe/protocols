## "Relationship counsellor for the human–AI relationship"

*Captured 2026-07-08. Filed as whimsical — but it's load-bearing whimsy.*

The joke lands because the thesis is literally true: there **is** a relationship (the third entity from
`../trust-and-the-third-entity.md`), and its characteristic failure mode is a *relationship* problem, not
a technical one. Miscalibrated trust, projection, taking a fluent error as betrayal, over-attachment
after a good streak — these are the presenting complaints of a couple, and they arise because natural
language *forces* the social read. So the counsellor framing isn't decoration; it's the most honest
plain-English description of what the practice actually fixes: it re-calibrates trust, surfaces the
assumptions each party brings, and builds a working agreement (the protocol) the pair can rely on.

What it gets right:

- **Names the relationship as the unit** — resolves the tension the naming thread kept hitting (effect on
  the human vs. the dyad). The counsellor works on the *relationship*.
- **Sovereignty-safe** — a good counsellor builds the couple's own capacity and then leaves; they don't
  create dependence on the counsellor. That's upskilling-not-debt, exactly.
- **Disarming + memorable** — cuts through "AI enablement" consultancy-speak; a civilian gets it instantly.

Where it's a hook, not a masthead:

- Risks reading **soft/gimmicky** to an enterprise buyer, and undersells the rigor (cognitive principles,
  applied research, the protocol library). Better as a **top-of-funnel metaphor / talk title / landing
  hook** than as the institute's name.
- The metaphor tilts toward *repair* (something's wrong, come fix it). The corpus is as much about
  *building well from the start* as about fixing a troubled pairing — so lean "couples' coach / working-
  agreement facilitator" over "we fix broken relationships" if it graduates.

Usable lines it suggests: *"trust issues with your AI? that's normal — and fixable."* / *"we don't
counsel you to trust it more or less; we help you build something worth trusting."* / *couples counselling
for you and the first tool that talks back.*
