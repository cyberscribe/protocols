# Premise — the north-star claim

*2026-07-08. The core positioning premise for the research thread and the venture. Grounds
`mission.md`, the research statement (`../README.md`), and the naming work (`naming-shortlist.md`).*

---

Human–AI interaction is a whole new game. It is **the first real tool that talks** — the first whose
interface is our own native language. That makes the interface at once the *most intuitive possible*
and one that carries hidden baggage: **anthropomorphic projection**, which produces cognitive
dissonance when the tool "hallucinates" — a fluent, confident error lands as betrayal rather than as a
tool fault. (The mechanism is worked out in `../trust-and-the-third-entity.md`: natural language is an
inherently social medium, so the social read is *forced*, and miscalibrated trust is a standing
property of the channel, not a user error to be corrected once.)

The premise:

> The technology will adapt, change form, and improve over time. But **best practices for human–AI
> interaction — grounded in cognitive principles and applied research — will become the new north star
> of effectiveness in knowledge work.**

The model is the volatile layer; the practice is the durable one. What compounds and persists across
model churn is the methodology of interaction, not any given system's fluency. This is why the research
object is a *protocol library* and why the venture teaches *practice* rather than tooling — and it is
the through-line the name must carry: point at the enduring practice layer, not the machine.

## Naming implication

The premise settles a tension the naming exploration kept surfacing. The subject is the **interaction**
(the relationship), and the differentiator is the **enduring, research-grounded practice** — so the
name should foreground *method/practice grounded in cognition*, with "protocols" as the durable object.
"Cognitive / cognition" is on-thesis here not as a narrowing but because the premise explicitly grounds
the practices in **cognitive principles**.

*Update: the practice layer this premise points at was subsequently articulated as a named methodology —
**DRIVE** (`methodology.md`).*
