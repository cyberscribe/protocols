# Beyond the Aspirin

### A counter-proposal of remedies for AI-driven knowledge collapse

*Transhuman Protocols research thread · June 2026 · companion to `knowledge-collapse-synthesis.md` (diagnosis) and `knowledge-collapse-assumptions-evidence.md` (evidence audit)*

---

## The problem with the prescription

Acemoglu, Kong & Ozdaglar diagnose a structural disease and prescribe an informational aspirin. Their two remedies are (1) **garbling** — deliberately adding noise to cap the precision of agentic recommendations, in an optimal two-phase moratorium-then-cap — and (2) **raising aggregation capacity I**. The second is sound and we keep it. The first is the problem.

Garbling has four weaknesses, and each points to a better instrument:

1. **It destroys surplus indiscriminately.** Making the AI worse for *everyone* forgoes the genuine static gains — including for the co-thinker and the novice for whom the tool is a complement (synthesis §6). It treats a complement and a substitute identically because it acts on the tool, not on the use.
2. **It treats the symptom, not the root.** The disease is an *uninternalised externality* plus the *enclosure* of the commons and the *incentive structure that sets the dial* (synthesis §5). Precision is the surface; incentives are the cause.
3. **It needs a regulator that can cap precision globally.** That actor is hard to constitute, easy to evade, and invites jurisdictional arbitrage — a country that garbles loses the race to one that doesn't (synthesis §9, the competition cuts both ways).
4. **It is a single point of failure.** One instrument, one dial, one chokepoint.

The counter-proposal keeps AKO's *dynamic insight* — that you may have to escape the collapse basin before you can settle into a steady state — but replaces the single suppressive lever with a layered set that **prices the cost, reshapes the tool, funds and diversifies the commons, anchors the institutions, and aligns the owner.**

---

## Six design principles

1. **Price, don't suppress.** Internalise the commons cost so the dial-setter feels it, rather than capping the output. (Pigouvian, not prohibitionist.)
2. **Shape, don't degrade.** The substitute/complement split is *endogenous and designable* (Bastani). Reshape the tool's form; don't lower its capability.
3. **Fund and diversify the commons.** Raise I — but as *partial* connectivity that preserves diversity, not a monoculture (Derex & Boyd non-monotonicity, synthesis §3/§6).
4. **Build on institutions that already partially internalise** — compulsory education, credentialing, inter-state competition — rather than inventing a global regulator (synthesis §9).
5. **Set the right scoreboard.** Anchor success metrics on *durable, unaided competence* and contribution, not tool-augmented output (synthesis §9 hinge).
6. **Align the owner with the whole.** Make the universal-owner internalisation real through broad ownership and pluralism (synthesis §5/§7).
7. **Shape the imagination.** What gets built is bounded by what can be pictured; cultivate protopian narratives that widen the space of buildable futures (Magnifica Humanitas ¶116; synthesis §4, §8). *(Added after the Public AI field check below surfaced a lever the first draft under-weighted.)*

## The remedy menu

Each instrument names the model lever it pulls, a real-world precedent, and its trade-offs.

### A. Price the externality (the move AKO omits)

**A1. A Pigouvian "commons-depletion" charge.** A levy on agentic-AI deployment scaled to its *substitution intensity*, earmarked to fund the commons (C) and contributor pools (A2).
- *Lever:* counters the DE-beats-IE asymmetry by making the diffuse, deferred cost *present and legible* to the deployer; achieves AKO's interior optimum τ⋆_A via **price rather than quantity**.
- *Why better than garbling:* a price instrument preserves the static surplus for high-value uses while still internalising the social cost; garbling destroys surplus indiscriminately (cf. Weitzman, prices vs. quantities). It is *targeted* — socially costly substitution pays; complementary, commons-feeding use is untouched or subsidised.
- *Precedent:* carbon pricing (cognitive-commons erosion as a "negative externality" analogue).
- *Trade-off:* measuring "substitution intensity" is genuinely hard (unlike carbon tonnes); risk of mismeasurement, pass-through to users, and arbitrage absent coordination.

**A2. Contributor compensation / commons royalties.** Route a share of model rents back to the humans whose effort the model enclosed (code, writing, Q&A, images).
- *Lever:* directly restores the λ_G incentive — re-feeds the commons (assumption 5), addressing the *dynamic* root rather than the symptom. Sustains contribution *alongside* high-precision AI (a both/and, not AKO's either/or).
- *Precedent:* performance-rights collectives (ASCAP/PRS), reprographic levies, the Alaska Permanent Fund as a dividend model.
- *Trade-off:* attribution at fine grain may be intractable; coarse pools risk unfairness and rent-seeking; ability-to-pay can entrench incumbents.

### B. Reshape the tool (complement-by-design)

**B1. Complement-design standards and procurement.** Public procurement and educational standards that require scaffolding / Socratic modes (withhold the answer, force the generation) in base-building domains.
- *Lever:* flips τ_A from substitute to complement *without lowering capability* — the Bastani GPT-Tutor arm, which eliminated the learning harm that the answer-serving arm caused.
- *Why better than garbling:* garbling makes the tool worse for all; complement-design keeps it powerful but pedagogically shaped.
- *Precedent:* safety certification, accessibility standards.
- *Trade-off:* only coherent where "an answer to withhold" is pedagogically meaningful; resisted by the commercial gradient; gameable.

**B2. Provenance and contribution loops.** Interfaces that surface reasoning, require user elaboration, cite sources, and re-feed verified user contributions back into the commons — turning *using* the AI into an act of *contributing* (closes the co-thinking → commons loop, synthesis §6).
- *Lever:* raises the extensive margin of commons production the model can't represent; converts private co-thinking into public λ_G.
- *Trade-off:* friction users may reject; quality control of re-fed contributions; homogenisation risk if everyone's contributions are model-shaped (manage via C2).

### C. Fund and diversify the commons (the no-regret lever, done right)

**C1. Public knowledge infrastructure.** Fund open datasets, public-interest models, libraries, and Wikipedia-class institutions as public goods.
- *Lever:* the unambiguously welfare-improving I (Props 9, 12) — the one lever with no static downside.
- *Precedent:* public libraries, public broadcasting, open science / NIH.
- *Trade-off:* sustained public funding against fiscal pressure; governance/capture.

**C2. Diversity protection (against homogenisation collapse).** Maintain plurality of models, sources, and curricula; antitrust against model monopoly; support minority-language, -discipline, and -culture knowledge bases.
- *Lever:* protects the *variance* the collective brain runs on — the Derex & Boyd result that *partial* connectivity out-innovates full connectivity (synthesis §3/§6). "Raise I" must mean raise connectivity **and** preserve diversity, or you trade one collapse for another.
- *Precedent:* media-plurality rules, antitrust, biodiversity/seed-bank logic applied to knowledge.
- *Trade-off:* in tension with the efficiency of a single shared resource; hard to measure "enough" diversity.

### D. Anchor the institutions (effort floors)

**D1. Mandated base-building in education.** Compulsory, effort-preserving curricula that maintain foundational X against the substitution gradient — the "we still drill arithmetic despite calculators" principle extended to judgment, synthesis, and writing-as-thinking.
- *Lever:* imposes the baseline learning the paper itself concedes "rule[s] out collapse" by reducing effective effort elasticity (assumption 8); the political-economy engine is inter-state competition (synthesis §9).
- *Trade-off:* selecting which X is foundational, with lag; risk that the institution fails to recognise the diffuse X that LLMs substitute in time (the two-clocks problem).

**D2. Credentialing / licensure requiring demonstrated *unaided* competence.** Bar-exam / board-certification / pilot-hours logic — the medical-training example AKO cites approvingly.
- *Lever:* institutional inelasticity of effort at the professional margin.
- *Trade-off:* credential inflation; gatekeeping/exclusion; can ossify.

### E. Align the owner (attack the concentration root)

**E1. Broad ownership of the AI capital stock.** Sovereign-wealth / citizen-equity stakes / public compute, so the body that captures the concentrated gain also bears the diffuse loss.
- *Lever:* makes the *universal-owner internalisation* real rather than theoretical (synthesis §7); directly addresses the r > g enclosure dynamic (§5).
- *Precedent:* Norway's sovereign wealth fund; Alaska Permanent Fund.
- *Trade-off:* enormous political resistance; governance risk (state as owner); does not by itself fix the cognitive externality.

**E2. Antitrust, data-portability, model pluralism.** Prevent enclosure *monopoly* (the "monopoly-not-capitalism" fork from §5).
- *Lever:* keeps the commons from being held by a few; complements C2.
- *Trade-off:* slow; jurisdictional; risk of fragmenting beneficial scale.

### F. Set the scoreboard (the hinge)

**F1. Re-anchor metrics on durable-X and dignity.** Measure unaided competence, contribution to the commons, and distribution — not GDP-now or tool-allowed test scores.
- *Lever:* the hinge on which §9's inter-state competition turns from collapse-accelerating to collapse-averting; operationalises the §8 dignity premise ("the value of persons does not depend on what they produce").
- *Trade-off:* politically hard; metrics are gameable and short-termist (the PISA dynamic).

### G. Shape the imagination (the narrative lever)

**G1. Protopian narrative and cultural production.** Commission and circulate "protopian" fiction and cultural artifacts — workable AI futures between techno-utopia and existential dread — to widen the space of buildable futures.
- *Lever:* operationalises the ¶116 / "race"-framing insight (synthesis §4, §8) — narratives shape the collective imagination and thereby the social, economic, and political choices that *set the dial*. Acts upstream of every other lever.
- *Precedent:* Public AI's Protopian AI Fiction Prize; the documented influence of science fiction on technologists' goals.
- *Trade-off:* diffuse, slow, hard to attribute causal impact; drifts into PR or utopianism if undisciplined.

## Refilling AKO's two-phase scaffold

The counter-proposal is not anti-AKO; it keeps the paper's dynamic logic — you may need to escape the collapse basin before settling — and refills both phases with better instruments:

| | AKO | Counter-proposal |
|---|-----|------------------|
| **Phase 1 (rebuild — escape the basin)** | Full suppression (moratorium) | Fund the commons (C1) + complement-design and effort floors in education (B1, D1) to rebuild X and lift the system past the basin threshold X̄ₘ |
| **Phase 2 (steady state)** | Permanent precision cap at τ⋆ | Pigouvian commons-charge (A1) + contributor compensation (A2) + diversity protection (C2) + broad ownership (E1) + dignity-anchored metrics (F1) as the durable regime |

Phase 1 is dispensable if the inherited stock of general knowledge is already above the basin threshold — exactly AKO's own condition.

## Why this beats the aspirin

| Criterion | Garbling | Counter-proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Static surplus | Destroyed indiscriminately | Preserved for high-value/complementary use |
| Targeting | Blunt (all users) | Targeted (socially costly use pays; complements untouched) |
| Treats | Symptom (precision) | Root (incentives, ownership, institutions) |
| Dynamic commons incentive | Restored only indirectly | Restored directly (A2 re-feeds λ_G) |
| Governance requirement | A global precision-capping regulator | Existing institutions (tax, education, antitrust, ownership) |
| Robustness | Single point of failure | Layered / defence-in-depth |
| Arbitrage exposure | High (evade by jurisdiction) | Lower (multiple independent levers) |

| Instrument | Authentic | Principled | Effective | Named gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Pigouvian charge | Med | **High** | Med | Measuring substitution intensity |
| A2 Contributor pay | **High** | **High** | Med | Fine-grain attribution |

| B2 Contribution loops | **High** | Med | Med | User friction; homogenisation |
| C1 Public infrastructure | **High** | **High** | **High** | Sustained funding |
| C2 Diversity protection | Med | **High** | Med | Hard to measure "enough" |
| D1 Mandated base-building | **High** | **High** | Med–High | Which X; lag |
| D2 Credentialing | Med | Med | Med | Inflation; exclusion |
| E1 Broad ownership | **High** | Med | Low–Med | Political resistance |
| E2 Antitrust/pluralism | Med | **High** | Med | Slow; jurisdictional |

The pattern: the instruments that score **Effective** today (B1, C1, D1) are the *least* dependent on new political settlements; the highest-ceiling instruments (A1, A2, E1, F1) are **Principled/Authentic** but gated on institutional and political will — the same will the diagnosis says is hard to summon (the collective-action trap, synthesis §4).

## Minimum viable bundle

If only three moves are feasible, choose the ones that are model-endorsed, have an existence proof, and need the least new political machinery:

1. **C1 — Fund and diversify public knowledge infrastructure.** No-regret; the one lever with no static downside; raises resilience directly.
2. **B1 + D1 — Complement-design plus mandated base-building in education.** Operationalises the institutional inelasticity the paper itself concedes prevents collapse; the calculator/arithmetic case is the existence proof.
3. **A1/A2 — A commons-charge that funds (1) and (2) and seeds contributor pools.** Internalises the root externality and makes the bundle self-financing.

Ownership (E1) and metric reform (F1) are higher-ceiling but slower; pursue them as the long game.

## Field check: Public AI Network as a partial instantiation

Public AI Network (Q4 2025 roundup, May 2026) is the strongest real-world instantiation to date of the *supply-side* quadrant of this proposal — and a useful test of where the framework's coverage is uneven.

| Public AI initiative | Lever | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Inference Utility, Swiss SPIU, "Airbus for AI" | C1 + E1/E2 (public, plural, multilateral compute) | Strong — the multilateral/consortium framing directly answers this proposal's *jurisdictional-arbitrage* failure mode |
| AI Commons, Concordance (ag-data pools) | C1 + C2 (de-enclose + community data pools) | Strong — near-verbatim on the §5 enclosure root |
| Community-aligned benchmarks, AI Evaluator Forum, Weval | F (scoreboard) | Partial — measures model capability/safety, not commons health |
| Public AI for Libraries | C1 access; latent B1/D site | Partial |
| Protopian AI Fiction Prize | G (imagination) | Strong — the instrument that prompted adding lever G |

**What it covers well:** C, E, F-partial, and G — the de-enclosure and public-infrastructure half. Its own framing ("the data layer of AI is being enclosed faster than it's being stewarded") is the §5 enclosure reading almost verbatim, and "Public AI as the new multilateralism" turns the §9 inter-state dynamic from race into cooperation.

**The structural blind spot (supply vs. demand).** Public AI is almost entirely *supply-side*: build public, open, plural infrastructure. But AKO's collapse engine is *demand-side* — individuals exerting less learning effort because AI substitutes for it. The network touches none of **A** (pricing/compensation), **B** (complement-design), or **D** (effort floors). A public inference utility is still an answer-server; by democratising capable models it may even *raise population-wide τ_A* — the substitution force — while fixing ownership. **Necessary infrastructure, not a sufficient remedy:** it fixes *who owns the commons and keeps it open and plural*, not *whether humans keep contributing to and learning from it.*

**The open-vs-compensate fork.** Public AI is firmly in the *open-commons* camp (keep the canon free; a copyright-sceptical reading list). That is in genuine tension with **A2** (meter-and-compensate). Both internalise the externality but pull opposite ways — free-and-public vs. price-and-pay. A real fault line in the remedy space; choose a side knowingly rather than assuming the levers compose.

**The missing metric.** Their eval infrastructure measures *model* capability and safety, not the AKO variable — X, the stock of general knowledge / human unaided competence. The **F1** metric that would actually track *collapse* (commons health, contribution rates, unaided performance) is unbuilt, by them and everyone. A concrete, adjacent gap a thoughtful actor could fill.

**Scale realism.** Right form, currently sub-scale against the private capex it counters (one inference utility, one national pilot, library pilots in three states, a fiction prize). A latent pull-point trying to activate — the synthesis's conditional-optimism verdict made flesh.

**A/P/E:** Authentic high, Principled high, Effective promising-but-unproven and *silent on the demand-side mechanism that drives the collapse it would prevent.* To bear on collapse specifically it would need pairing with complement-design in its library/education deployments (B1), an explicit effort-floor/literacy mission (D1), and a commons-health metric (F1).

## Failure modes and the strongest objections (defensibility)

A counter-proposal is only defensible if it names where it breaks:

- **Attribution may be intractable.** A2's contributor compensation can degrade into coarse, captured, rent-seeking pools if fine-grain attribution proves impossible.
- **Pigouvian measurement.** A1 needs a credible metric of commons-erosion; "cognitive emissions" are far harder to count than carbon, and a mismeasured price misallocates.
- **Complement-design has limited domain.** B1 only bites where withholding an answer is pedagogically coherent; much real use isn't like that.
- **Effort floors select and lag (synthesis §9).** D1 may preserve the wrong X, or recognise the right one too late.
- **Jurisdictional arbitrage.** Unilateral A1/E2 invite a race to the laxer regime — the §9 competition dynamic turned against us. Requires coordination the world is bad at.
- **Capture and incumbency.** Several instruments (A2, C1, E1) can be captured by the very actors they aim to discipline.
- **The libertarian/efficiency objection.** A principled critic will argue all of this dampens a technology delivering large absolute welfare gains, that the externality is overstated, and that markets/competition (not intervention) will surface the complement-design that wins. This deserves a real answer, not dismissal: the case for intervention rests on the externality being *real, dynamic, and uninternalised* — which the evidence audit supports but does not make certain.
- **It is institutional, not automatic.** The whole package needs sustained political will. Its honest status is the synthesis's conditional-optimism verdict: *the pull-points exist and are currently too weak to fire on their own; the work is strengthening them faster than the substitute-gradient compounds.*

**Net.** Against AKO's single suppressive dial, this is a layered programme that prices the cost, reshapes the tool, funds and diversifies the commons, anchors the institutions, and aligns the owner — preserving the static gains AKO throws away while attacking the root AKO leaves untouched. It is more efficient, more robust, and more honest about depending on institutions rather than a regulator. It is also harder, and it should be argued for as such.
