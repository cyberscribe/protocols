# The externalisation simulation

**Nothing in this document has been run.** It is a proposed model, written up so that its coherence can be attacked before any of it is built. §8–§10 are where it is weakest and are the reason for circulating it now.

---

## 1. The question

A knowledge worker using an AI assistant gets better outcomes immediately. A growing experimental literature finds they also get worse at the work when the assistance is removed — after minutes, in the lab. Nobody has shown whether that task-level effect *aggregates* into anything over months of professional practice, and nobody has shown which working practices, if any, prevent it.

The specific question here is narrower and mechanical:

> Two things move information across the human–AI boundary. Context flows into each session automatically and for free. Judgment flows back out into storage only when a person deliberately puts it there. **Given that asymmetry, what happens to the person's calibration and capability over time, and does the deliberate direction protect them?**

---

## 2. Why a simulation

The claim to be tested is about a feedback loop with a long delay, and loops with long delays are exactly where verbal reasoning fails. Three specific things need a model rather than an argument:

1. The proposed harm mechanism is **self-concealing** — the faculty that would detect it is the faculty it degrades. Verbal arguments of that shape are easy to construct and impossible to check.
2. The intervention (writing judgment back to storage) **builds the thing that causes the harm** — a larger store means more automatic loading. Whether the intervention is net positive is not decidable by inspection.
3. Outcome feedback in professional work arrives after long, variable delay, and delay changes whether a control loop settles or oscillates. That is a quantitative question with a qualitative answer.

**What the simulation is not.** It produces no evidence about the world. It is an instrument for finding out whether a set of stated assumptions can produce a stated behaviour, and which assumptions the behaviour actually depends on. No number from it should appear anywhere as a measurement.

---

## 3. The setup, in plain terms

A worker does a stream of tasks with an AI assistant over a long horizon.

**A store** holds accumulated working context — standards, references, templates, prior decisions. Its contents are pulled into every task automatically, at no cost and with no decision.

**Writing back** is the only thing the worker chooses. After a task they may spend effort recording what actually mattered: the criteria used, assumptions made, why the decision went that way, what was checked, what would change their mind. Not a transcript — judgment-bearing material.

**Two things the worker does are tracked separately**, and keeping them separate is the crux of the design:

- **reasoning** — how much of the problem they worked out themselves before asking
- **writing back** — how much judgment-bearing material they recorded afterwards

These are different activities with different drivers, and conflating them is the main way this model could fail (§10, W1).

**Three stocks change slowly:** the worker's capability, their trust in the assistant, and the store — which has both *mass* (how much is in it) and *validity* (how much of it is still true).

---

## 4. What this inherits

The project already contains a validated engine (`trust-trap`) reproducing a load/trust/debt limit cycle, and a reading layer (`posture-machine`) that quantises continuous engine variables into a discrete state machine via Schmitt triggers with a deadband. This model reuses the reading layer and the shared parameter values but **not** the state vector: its `sovereignty` is not this model's `reasoning`, and its `debt` is not the complement of this model's `capability`.

Empirical anchors, all verified against source:

- **Chen et al. 2026** (*Human Factors*): trust updates through two pathways — process evidence and outcome evidence — and cognitive load shifts the relative weight from the first to the second. The paper's own framing.
- **Bastani et al. 2025** (PNAS, preregistered, ~1,000 students): unguarded GPT access improved practice performance 48% and *reduced* unaided exam performance 17%; a guardrailed version improved practice 127% with no exam penalty.
- **Wu et al. 2026** (HCOMP): independent problem-solving effort predicts latent-ability gain; **AI request frequency does not**, once initial ability and independent effort are controlled. The authors flag this as associational.

Wu is the load-bearing one for the model's structure: it supports *assistance harms skill when it displaces reasoning*, **not** *assistance harms skill holding reasoning constant*. The model is built accordingly — loading has no direct penalty and harms only by displacement.

## 5. The model

Discrete time. `logistic(x) = 1/(1+e^{-x})`. All state variables clamped to `[0,1]`.

### 5.1 Exogenous

| symbol | meaning | form |
|---|---|---|
| `B_t` | the assistant's true reliability on this task | AR(1), φ = 0.80, mean 0.55, uniform shock ±0.10, clamped [0.30, 0.80] |
| `U_t` | whether this period contains an unaided probe | indicator |

`B` is known to the model and never to the simulated worker. Everything the worker believes is inferred.

### 5.2 The two behaviours

```
H_t = logistic( h0 − h_T·T_t − h_L·L_t + h_ρ·ρ + h_Z·Z )              writing back
R_t = logistic( r0 − r_T·T_t − r_L·L_t − r_Λ·Λ_t + r_Z·Z )            reasoning
```

**`Λ` enters reasoning and never writing-back. `ρ` enters writing-back and never reasoning.** This is the single most important structural decision in the model. Both behaviours fall with trust and load and rise with observability; if that were all they had they would be collinear and the state space would collapse from eight cells to four. Loading displaces reasoning (Wu) but does not displace recording; discipline/capacity `ρ` sustains recording but is not what makes you think.

### 5.3 Flows and stocks

```
Λ_t     = X_m · relevance(q_t)                                        loading: free, automatic
L_{t+1} = L_t + λ_L·( c_L·(1 − Λ_t) − L_t )                           load falls as loading rises
W_t     = Z · H_t                                                     writing back; zero when H = 0
X_m'    = (1 − δ_X)·X_m + f·W_t                                       store mass
X_v'    = [ (1−δ_X)·X_m·X_v·(1−δ_v) + f·W_t ] / X_m'                  store validity, mass-weighted
```

New material enters at validity 1; unrefreshed material loses validity at `δ_v` while keeping its mass. **Mass is what gets loaded.** A store that stops being maintained is therefore loaded into every session at full volume and declining truth — which is the mechanism by which debt accumulates in the *artefact* rather than the person.

### 5.4 Evidence, gated by capability

```
avail_t = 1 − (1 − Z)·(1 − H_t)                                       noisy-OR
leg_t   = avail_t · K_t                                               legibility gate
P_t     = leg_t·B_t + (1 − leg_t)·T_t                                 process evidence
```

Process evidence has to be both *produced* (by the harness, or by the act of writing back) and *legible* (which takes residual capability). When illegible it **echoes the prior rather than misleading** — it fails to correct, it does not actively deceive. This is deliberately the conservative choice: it means the model cannot manufacture overconfidence out of confusion, and any miscalibration it produces must come from somewhere identifiable.

### 5.5 Outcome

```
human_t = R_t · K_t
O_t     = min( cap, B_t·(1 + b·Λ_t·(2·X_v − 1)) + η_h·human_t )
```

Store validity above 0.5 improves outcomes; below 0.5 it **degrades** them. A stale store is worse than no store.

### 5.6 Trust

```
α_t     = logistic( a0 − a_L·L_t + a_K·K_t )                          pathway allocation
T_{t+1} = T_t + η·[ α_t·(P_t − T_t) + (1 − α_t)·(O_{t−τ} − T_t) ]
```

A strict simplex: `α` allocates between the two pathways rather than scaling them independently. Load pushes weight toward outcome; capability pulls it back toward process. Outcome evidence arrives `τ` periods late.

**The fixed point is `T* = α·P + (1−α)·O`, so miscalibration against process evidence is `(1−α)(O − P)`** — the outcome–process gap times the load-driven weight on outcome. No irrationality is required anywhere: both signals are honest, and the gap exists because substitutive assistance genuinely produces good outcomes.

### 5.7 Capability

```
K_{t+1} = (1 − δ_K)·K_t + γ·( R_t + κ·H_t )·(1 − K_t)                 κ < 1
```

Diminishing returns to a ceiling. Reasoning dominates; writing back contributes separately. **The `κ·H` term is this project's hypothesis and not Wu's finding** — Wu measures reasoning, not recording — and must not acquire a citation by proximity.

### 5.8 Measurement, which is not dynamics

```
K^obs_t = K_t + ν_t          only where U_t = 1
D_t     = K*_t − K^obs_t     readout, never fed back
```

`K*` runs the same capability equation at a reference reasoning level with `Λ = 0`: the counterfactual capability of the same worker without substitutive assistance. `U_t` also enters `R_t`, because a probe *is* a period of unaided practice — the instrument perturbs what it measures, and the model says so.

### 5.9 Parameters

Inherited unchanged from the validated engine, on the principle that a successor which cannot reproduce the established cycle is not a successor: `τ = 28`, `η = 0.18`, `δ_X = 0.02`, `f = 0.10`, `λ_L = 0.03`, `cap = 1.00`, `η_h = 0.90`, horizon 10,000 with 2,000 burn-in, quantiser deadband 0.30.

New and to be calibrated: `h*`, `r*`, `a*`, `γ`, `κ`, `δ_K`, `δ_v`, `b`, `c_L`. Procedure: fit the baseline to reproduce the known cycle, then sweep every parameter ±30% and **report the fraction of that space in which the headline result survives, on the figure itself.**

## 6. What is read out

Three binary readings per period, taken through the existing Schmitt-trigger quantiser (deadband 0.30, thresholds **frozen against a no-promotion baseline** rather than self-calibrated — a run where nobody ever writes back would otherwise read as ~50% "writing back" and destroy the comparison):

| bit | quantity | threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **W** — writing back | `H_t` | frozen baseline median |
| **R** — reasoning | `R_t` | frozen baseline median |
| **C** — calibrated | `\|T_t − B̄_t\|` | see immediately below |

**On the calibration bit, and this is a correction made while writing this up.** Calibration must be measured against the assistant's *typical* reliability, not its reliability on the current draw. `B` is AR(1) with φ = 0.80, so it has a correlation time of about five periods, while trust responds to outcomes 28 periods old. Measured against instantaneous `B`, trust could essentially never track it, the C bit would be near-always false, and the state space would degenerate to four cells for an entirely uninteresting reason. `B̄_t` is therefore a trailing mean of `B` over a window comparable to `τ`.

Eight cells. The output is which are occupied and which are never reached.

---

## 7. What the model is predicted to show

In descending order of confidence.

**P1 — the displacement signature.** Raising `Λ` raises `H` and lowers `R` *in the same run*. Loading relieves load; writing back is easier when less loaded; but loading also displaces reasoning. So a better-provisioned worker records more and thinks less. **On the reading a manager takes — is the documentation current? — they improve. On the reading that matters they degrade.** This follows directly from the sign structure and should be robust.

**P2 — promotion is self-undermining.** Writing back grows the store; a larger store loads more; more loading displaces reasoning. Nobody put this in deliberately; it falls out of `X_m → Λ → R`. It implies an interior optimum in writing-back effort, which is a *different* question from the fixed-policy optimum this project abandoned earlier: that one asked which constant attention level wins in a limit-cycling system, this one is a structural trade-off within a single channel.

**P3 — store-side debt separates from person-side debt.** A run with high `X_m` and low `X_v` should be distinguishable on outcomes from a run with low `X_m`. This is the failure mode where the artefact rots rather than the person, and no existing model in this project or in the literature represents it.

**P4 — the empty cell.** `(¬W, ¬R, C)` — not writing back, not reasoning, still calibrated — is never occupied. **This is the weakest of the four and §8 explains why.**

---

## 8. Where P4 actually comes from, and why it is uncertain

A reviewer's first question should be whether the empty cell is baked in. Tracing it honestly:

With `H = 0` and `R = 0`: capability decays; as `K → 0`, legibility `→ 0` and process evidence collapses to the prior, so trust tracks outcome alone. Store validity decays. Outcome tends to `B·(1 − b·Λ)`. Trust therefore converges to something below `B̄`, and the C bit goes false.

**So P4 depends on `b > 0` and `δ_v > 0`.** If a decaying store did not bias outcomes, outcomes would be an unbiased signal of `B`, trust would converge on `B̄`, and a worker who neither reasons nor records would be *perfectly calibrated* — idle, deskilled, and correct about the tool. **The empty cell is not a logical consequence of the setup; it is a consequence of a substantive assumption about stale context degrading results.** That assumption is stated, defensible and testable, and it should be attacked directly rather than smuggled.

**There is also a second, competing miscalibration mechanism running the other way.** `O` is capped. A worker with high reasoning and high capability pushes `B + η_h·R·K` above the cap, so their observed outcomes saturate and stop reflecting `B` — the same headroom mechanism the project's earlier engine used to hide deterioration. That produces miscalibration at *high* effort.

So the model contains one mechanism miscalibrating the disengaged and another miscalibrating the highly engaged, and **calibration may turn out to be best in the middle.** That would be a more interesting result than P4 and it would put the empty cell somewhere other than predicted. It is a genuine open question about the model's behaviour, which is why occupancy is a gate rather than a claim: **if all eight cells are occupied, there is no structural finding, and that is the result that gets reported.**

## 9. Prior expectations to check the build against

Before any new claim, the model must reproduce what is already established:

1. A limit cycle survives with all randomness removed.
2. Longer feedback delay destabilises: for the linearised outcome channel the tolerable gain falls roughly as `1/τ`. *(This boundary is quoted from the standard scalar delay-recursion result and has not been computed for this system — see §10, W4.)*
3. Guardrails help: a run with high `Z` should reproduce the qualitative Bastani pattern — outcomes rise, unaided capability does not fall.

## 10. Where a reviewer should press

| # | weakness |
|---|---|
| **W1** | **Separability of `H` and `R`.** The whole eight-cell structure requires that writing-back and reasoning do not collapse into one variable. They share three drivers and differ in one each. Whether that is enough separation is an empirical property of the fitted system, not a guarantee. `\|corr(H,R)\|` bounded away from 1 across the swept space is a gate condition; it is untested. |
| **W2** | **`κ·H` in the capability equation is unsupported.** It is this project's hypothesis that recording judgment builds capability. Wu measures reasoning. If `κ = 0` the model still works and the claim about promotion weakens to "preserves evidence" rather than "preserves evidence and capability." |
| **W3** | **`P` as the calibration target in the identity, `B̄` as the target for the bit.** These are two different notions of calibration used in two places. That is deliberate — the gap between them is the thesis — but it is a place where an argument could be quietly switched between senses. |
| **W4** | **The delay-stability boundary is quoted, not derived for this system.** The full model is not scalar. |
| **W5** | **`ρ` is exogenous.** Discipline/capacity is the one driver that sustains writing-back, and it is treated as a constant per run. If discipline itself erodes under load or trust, `H` loses its distinguishing driver and W1 bites. |
| **W6** | **Every empirical anchor is short-horizon, lab or classroom, with the assistant as a solution-provider on checkable problems.** The model's target is professionals on ill-specified problems over months. That the mechanism *aggregates* is the contribution and is untested by anyone. |
| **W7** | **One store, not four.** Real context stores differ in loading discipline — some always loaded, some per-project. Collapsing them to a single stock with a validity dimension may hide a mechanism, specifically that the *most* loaded store is the *least* reviewed. |

---

## 11. Falsification

- **Gate:** if `|corr(H,R)| → 1` anywhere in the swept space, the state space has collapsed and the design is wrong (W1).
- **Gate:** if all eight cells are occupied under baseline, there is no structural finding. Report and stop.
- **P1 fails** if raising `Λ` moves `H` and `R` the same way — the displacement story is then wrong.
- **P3 fails** if store mass and store validity cannot be distinguished on outcomes.
- **Robustness:** if the occupancy result does not survive ±30% variation in every parameter, it is a parameter artefact and belongs in an instrument, never in a figure.

**The rail:** the three bits are fixed by what the model says is happening. They are not to be adjusted until a cell empties. If the predicted hole does not appear, that is a finding about the model.

---

## 12. Deliberate exclusions

**Concurrency** — the project's prior attempts failed by carrying concurrency, temperament, tooling and aids simultaneously; isolating one mechanism is what worked.
**Four separate stores** — see W7.
**An optimising agent** — the search for a fixed-policy optimum was closed on the grounds that a fixed point is the wrong question in a limit-cycling system. The agent here is behavioural.
**Game theory** — there is no second player. The nearest formal parent is costly state verification (Townsend 1979) with the novelty that *choosing not to verify degrades the ability to verify*. That deserves its own treatment and is not this.
