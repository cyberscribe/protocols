Session plumbing · where knowledge gets sorted

Unscrambling the omelette

Knowledge leaves a working session entangled — one undifferentiated mixture. Separating it into the things it is actually made of is the work, and it is the only place the person gets anything back.

Working memory is the only vessel with a hole in it.

Three ways it goes wrong

Fabricate. Bogus traces arrive from outside and fly straight into a store. They never pass the gate, so nothing checks them. Watch which side of the picture they come from.

Drop. Good work is plucked out of the flow before it lands. Nothing is added and nothing looks wrong — the store just quietly stops representing what actually happened.

Tamper. The content is bad and the label says clean. The gate can only read the label. Turn this on and watch the catch rate collapse while the traffic looks identical.

Read the ring, not the dot

Every particle carries two things: a core, which is what it actually is, and a ring, which is what it claims to be. Normally they agree.

Tampering breaks them apart — a red core inside a clean ring. Every defence in the picture reads the ring. That is why tampering is the dangerous one and why it is the attack we have never modelled: it does not corrupt the store, it corrupts the thing we check with.

Separation is the work

Particles leave the session grey. They acquire their colour in the separator, not before — because what kind of thing a fragment is gets decided in the act of sorting it. That is why promotion is effortful and why it cannot be handed over wholesale.

Switch to Let it flow through and the stores still fill. They fill with grey.

Two streams come back to you

A thin mixed stream — rehearsal, a sample of everything you sorted, the by-product of having handled it.

And a full stream of working standards, which is a different thing entirely: standards ground you, scaling the exchange to terms you already hold. General reference never comes back — it only ever travels toward the AI. That is what separates those two types, and it is a difference in plumbing, not in policy.

The leak has no valve

Working memory drains continuously, whether or not anything changed in the world. Every other vessel here only goes wrong when the world moves — and one write repairs it.

Turn separation off and watch which vessel empties. Nothing else does.

Status is drawn off, not sent up

The volatile fractions — status, and to a lesser degree projects — stay on your side and near the top. They are not context that hasn’t been written down yet.

Sending a transient fraction down into the durable vessels is not untidiness. It is how the durable vessels get contaminated.

The three attacks are from Zhong & Evans, When Ants Attack: Security Issues for Stigmergic Systems (UVA CS, 2001), which studies ant-colony routing — an analogy for this, not evidence about people. Our own model represents only the first of the three. Vessel fill levels are illustrative, not model output: inflow is set by the sorting rate and working memory drains at a fixed rate standing in for autonomous decay. The claim being drawn is structural — which streams exist, which direction they run, and which vessel leaks.
