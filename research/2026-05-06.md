## B2 — Protocols and results need to be inspectable

- Introspection adapters: a single LoRA adapter trained on a population of fine-tuned models causes them to self-report learned behaviors in natural language; SOTA on auditing benchmark; detects encrypted fine-tuning attacks — makes *learned* (not just executed) behavior reportable. — [anthropic-alignment-introspection-adapters-training-llms-to-report](read/2026-05-05-anthropic-alignment-introspection-adapters-training-llms-to-report.md)
- Activation oracles: models trained to answer questions about their own activations in natural language ("is the model uncertain here?") — bridges interpretability to live agent oversight. — [transformer-circuits-oracles](read/2026-05-04-transformer-circuits-oracles.md)
- HeadVis complicates naïve B2: "a head's behavior on the full distribution rarely matches what a narrow task suggests" — inspection itself misleads if the test set is narrow. - METR red-teaming of Anthropic's internal agent monitoring found novel vulnerabilities under three weeks of access — establishes external adversarial review as a meta-protocol on the inspection layer itself. — [metr-red-teaming-anthropic-s-internal-agent](read/2026-05-05-metr-red-teaming-anthropic-s-internal-agent.md)

## B3 — Human constraints (cognition, attention, time, energy)

- Shaw & Nave (Wharton): 1,372 participants, 9,593 trials, 3 experiments, **16× cognitive-surrender effect** when AI assistance is available — the first hard empirical anchor for what was previously framed as automation bias / cognitive offloading. — [datachutney-cognitive-surrender-explainer](read/2026-05-05-datachutney-cognitive-surrender-explainer.md)
- Mollick: cognitive load measured turn-by-turn on financial professionals using GPT-4o — productivity gains were partly offset because "the AI presented information in a way that completely overwhelmed people: giant walls of text, offers to pursue new topics." Less-experienced workers were hurt most. *(small-N study)* — [ethan-mollick-claude-dispatch](read/2026-05-04-ethan-mollick-claude-dispatch.md)

## B4 — Humans hold context AI won't have without supplying it

Protocols must preserve context routes from human-held situation to AI-acted-on data. *(practitioner)* — [charity-majors-your-data-is-made-powerful-by](read/2026-05-05-charity-majors-your-data-is-made-powerful-by.md)
