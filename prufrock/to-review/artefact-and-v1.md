# Artefact, Named Deliverable, Institutional Pairing, Scoped v1

The protocol is the engine, not the deliverable. Long Now backs objects with custodianship — the Clock has Nevada, the Rosetta Disk has its institutional partners, Long Bets has the registry. Here's what survives the talk.

## Named deliverable

***Continual Proof: Volume I*** — a hand-bound print codex (edition of 250) containing the first season's fourteen sonnets, fourteen poets, fourteen days, with maps of where in the world each line was written, the precomputed assignment matrix revealed in the colophon, and a tipped-in QR pointing to the live ledger and on-chain inscriptions. Title from Stephen Dunn's "Different Hours": *Bring to me, it said, continual proof / you've been alive*. The codex is the readable demonstration of the project's wager: that poetry — the oldest long-now form — can be enlisted as the timepiece for a new kind of time, at once unique to each signed moment and continuous with the timeless themes the form has always engaged.

## Durable artefact (across pace layers)

The codex is the readable surface, but the work is anchored at three speeds at once — Stewart Brand's pace-layer framework expressed as media:

- **Operational (daily):** the public web ledger and signing app — every line viewable, cross-referenceable, navigable across geographic, temporal, and dialectical lenses.
- **Annual:** the print codex, deposited canonically in the Long Now Foundation Library and at The Interval, dated in Long Now Zero (02028, 02029, …).
- **Per-line and millennial:** each line individually inscribed as a Bitcoin Ordinal at the moment of signing — the "sovereign time" anchor named in the callout — with a planned decennial Rosetta-class nickel-disk etching of each decade's spine for ten-thousand-year readability.

The point isn't redundancy but pace-layer coherence: the same protocol producing artefacts at fashion, commerce, and culture speeds, mutually reinforcing.

## Institutional pairing

- **Long Now Foundation** — canonical host of the print codex and the project's home within the Protocols for the Long Now lab series.
- **Protocols Institute** — protocol design and validation; convenes the protocol-theory cohort for the Year 1 trial.
- **The Poetry Society (UK)** and **Kundiman (US)** — literary anchors for the poet cohort; both prior partners of Transatlantic Poetry.
- **Long Bets** — a registered Long Bet at trial close (e.g. *"By 02050, a Prufrock-protocol chain will be canonised in a major literary anthology"*) to give the project a public adversarial check at Long Now's own institutional layer.

## Scoped v1 (Spring Equinox 02028)

Funded August 02026 → built through 02026–02027 → trial autumn 02027 → codex Spring Equinox 02028 (one Long Now Zero year after the *Book of Time* anthology launch).

- Open-source mobile app: interruption, presentation, signing
- Public web ledger: live during trial, indexed thereafter
- 14-poet × 14-day trial executed across at least 5 time zones
- Bitcoin Ordinal inscription per signed line
- *Continual Proof: Volume I* — bound print codex, edition of 250
- One ~3-minute film-poem assembled from the trial's optional photo/video attachments
- Public unveiling at The Interval, in which the assignment matrix is unsealed against its prior cryptographic commitment

## Deferred to v2+

- Annual codex production cadence (Volume II, III, …)
- Decennial nickel-disk etching of spines (the "millennial" layer)
- Expanded participant pool: protocol theorists, non-literary communities, communities new to poetry
- Cross-language extension (v1 is English-only by trial design)
- Web reader v2 with live geographic / temporal / dialectical visualisation
