AuthenticEliot's actual palette: yellow smoke, tea-stained ivory, drawing-room dusk, the peach he dares to eat. The poem isn't decoration here — it's the colour key.
PrincipledStrong thematic argument; the rose/mauve risks reading decorative if used at scale. Keep mauve as deep accent, not surface.
EffectiveThe most distinctive of the four. Memorable in a deck of pitches that all use slate-and-amber.

AuthenticThe register Eliot was actually published in: Faber & Faber, mid-century type, austere paper. Authentic to the book, not the poem.
PrincipledHardest to date. No metallic, no trend pigment. A single madder red carries every emphasis the artefact needs.
EffectiveMost "this will be taken seriously." Risk: visually quiet for a simulator UI; may need typography to do work the colour won't.
