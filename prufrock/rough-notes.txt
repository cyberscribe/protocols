Prufrock Protocol

Poetry is the one of the longest of long nows in human history, charting timeless themes (long) through concrete moments (nows) of individual expression. It is a set of protocols that continually reinvent themselves, proceeding linearly through contemporary context and individual consciousness yet folding time at pinch points into recurrent human themes. As such, poetry is a latent timepiece we can surface and utilise to understand humanity in both specific and timeless dimensions.



While LLMs create a regression to the mean of human concerns, poetry is the opposite -- an expansion of this "humanity" in aggregate into momentary instances--thus the "infinite game" of using words to transcend words, giving specificity to the timeless.

The Prufrock Protocol is also a provocation by which its eponymous figure  might finally act, finding transcendence in the midst of "coffee spoon" time. Where Prufrock requires courage to disturb the universe, and lacks it, the protocol brings disturbance to the willing participant, and compels them to act in the moment.
