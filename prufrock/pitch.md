# The Prufrock Protocol

> "I have measured out my life in coffee spoons"
>     -T.S. Eliot, "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock"

## The Problem

In a world forever changed by generative AI, where words and images can be synthesised from aggregations quickly, easily, and at scale, how can we continue to record authentic moments of human expression over time? 

One answer is to retreat to private and analogue approaches, such as a handwritten paper diary. Though more verifiable and incorruptible, these methods abandon the advances that digitisation and network distribution afford us for communicating with others quickly and globally, as well as for archiving artefacts at scale. 

Rather than throwing the baby out with the bath water, The Prufrock Protocol attempts to transplant individual human expression into a high-tech vessel filled with the timeless waters of poetry. In doing so, it presents a novel approach to marking time, sampling and recording subjective experience longitudinally in a authenticated format.

## The Protocol

The Prufrock Protocol measures time through randomly interrupting a moment in an individual's life, confronting them with a response to someone else's prior moment of interruption and confrontation, and recording the individual's response. This response forms the basis for further interruption and confrontation, as well as a durable record of the cascading exchange. The record also preserves time and location, and is cryptographically signed.

## The Bathtub

Most cryptographic ledgers attempt to solve issues of financial trust. Prufrock explores its use in authenticating expressions of situated human experience. Ironically, the need is more urgent: the collapse of centralised banking systems remains theoretical; the collapse of our trust in words and images has arrived.

## Poetry

The responses are framed as lines of poetry, and the durable record is a poem. This contextualises both the prompt and the response, giving the participant clear direction to experience of being confronted with a line of poetry at a random moment in their day.

## Compression Counterbalance

Poetry is perhaps our oldest compression algorithm. It distills subjective moments of individual reflection, originally into proscribed forms, now by recalling their echoes. Generative AI models also compress human culture, but do so statistically and anonymously, deriving patterns of association from past human artefacts.

Ipso facto, both compression methodologies lose fidelity along the way.

"The moment" of individual experience situated in time is one of the elements generative models sacrifice in favour of associational patterns; a timestamp or a person's name is just another (optional) variable. In poetry, it is the central element preserved, making tradeoffs instead that create space for inference and interpretation within the consciousness of the reader. This space is precisely what generative models attempt to fill with (statistically) quantifiable certainty, whereas poetry attempts instead to preserve its framing as negative space.

The Prufrock Protocol juxtaposes these forms of compression as complimentary, situating itself as antidote to the present hegemony of "anti-poetic" AI.

## An Enduring Artefact

Each poem as a chain of call-and-response becomes a novel cultural artifact. It records the dialectical progression of line (thesis) plus moment (antithesis) into new line (synthesis). It does so globally and longitudinally through verified responses to micro-moments over time. It is the left foot - right foot march of human experience, sampled randomly and individually, compressed through the lens of poetry, and validated through technology.

Although inspired by collaborative writing exercises such as the "exquisite corpse", the introduction of random interruption and confrontation as well as verified human authorship transcends this original inspiration. It is an experiment in marking time through moments of attention, reflection, and response. It is also a timely counterpoise to the erosion of authenticity in digital shared experiences caused by generative AI.

## The Experiment

A prototype would use the ubiquitous mobile smartphone as primary mechanism to transmit interruption, confrontation, and re-contribution. The initial experiment would engage existing communities of practice from the discipline of poetry as well other communities such as researchers in protocol theory with no sustained prior experience of poetry.

The results could be surfaced to be explored textually or visually through multiple dimensions: geographical, temporal, dialectic, thematic, climatic, and more. They can also form the basis for AI-based analysis of a human-only shared contribution space unpolluted by generative AI. In this way, The Prufrock Protocol may provide a means to preserving such spaces beyond this watershed moment, into a future where pristine pools of truly solely human-originated expression may become endangered.

## Questions

Where poem-ledgers begin or end, who participates in which ones, and what specific mechanisms transmit, receive, and store them all remain open questions to be resolved in specific detail, guided by the overall aims of the project.

The overarching question remains compelling, and clear: The Prufrock Protocol asks whether, in an era of synthetic media and accelerated attention, we might still construct durable shared memory from small moments of authentic human expression brought together as a new means of marking time.
