# Prufrock Protocol — Screencast Demo Script
## 2–3 minutes · narrated walkthrough

---

### SCREEN 1 — `01-seed-panel.html` · ~25 sec

*Open directly to this screen. Pause one beat before speaking.*

The Prufrock Protocol begins with a poem in progress.

Each poet is assigned a poem and given a prompt: the two most recent lines. Their new line must respond to what's already there. When they submit it, the time and location are captured automatically — proof of the moment of writing. Respond when you're ready. Midnight is the hard expiration.

*Let the screen sit. Then move.*

---

### SCREEN 2 — `05-lineage-path.html` · ~25 sec

*Click Lineage tab. Watch the trail animate — pause until nodes finish cascading.*

This is the lineage view for a single participant — Amara, writing poem seven of fourteen. The trail shows every poem in the sequence she's read before sitting down. Her choices are informed by the whole chain. The protocol isn't just a prompt; it's a conversation with everyone who wrote before her that day.

---

### SCREEN 3 — `06-map-full.html` · ~30 sec

*Click Map tab. Let animation run — seed arcs first, then Poem 01 handoffs. Don't talk over the first few seconds.*

*(wait for seed arcs to appear)*

The protocol is distributed. Each arc is a poem propagating outward — from the seed, to a poet, across continents.

*(as Poem 01 handoffs draw)*

These are the Poem 01 handoffs: one poet passes their signed work to the next in the chain, wherever they happen to be. No central platform, no single point of failure. A protocol, not a service.

*Click the ★ to replay if needed.*

---

### SCREEN 4 — `07-poem-sidebyside.html` · ~25 sec

*Click Poem tab.*

And here's what the poems look like. Left column: a 2026 poem from the first experiment. Right column: a projected response from a future cohort in 2126, who will have received lines seven and eight — sealed today — as their seed.

*Click line 8 on the right ("The patient cold is gone…") to show the annotation tooltip.*

The echo isn't a coincidence. It's the protocol working: a line written now, passed forward a hundred years, transformed by a poet who couldn't have known what it would mean by then.

---

### SCREEN 5 — `10-concerns-network.html` · ~25 sec

*Click Concerns tab. Watch edge cascade animate.*

Across all the poems, concerns emerge — themes running through individual works that no single poet planned. Identity. Memory. Climate arriving as something new. The network shows which themes run through which poems: a collective unconscious made visible, and verifiable.

---

### SCREEN 6 — `11-long-view.html` · ~20 sec

*Click Long view button (top-right button group). Watch ribbon reveal.*

Step back. These streams are the same themes, now rendered across the full arc of the experiment — thickening where more poets engage a concern, thinning where it recedes. Each ribbon is a thread of attention, held across time.

---

### SCREEN 7 — `12b-deep-time.html` · ~20 sec

*Click Deep Time button (top-right button group). Watch 6-second river reveal.*

Deepest zoom. The 2026 experiment is one thin stripe at the left. The protocol is designed to run indefinitely — seeding new cohorts across generations, each one receiving a fragment of what came before. The rivers are the concerns that survive. The gaps are where attention was lost.

---

### CLOSE — hold on `12b-deep-time.html` · ~15 sec

*Don't navigate away. Let the image hold.*

This is what poetry has always done: passed attention forward through time. The Prufrock Protocol makes that inheritance explicit, cryptographically certain, and open to anyone willing to sit with two lines and a blank line to follow.
