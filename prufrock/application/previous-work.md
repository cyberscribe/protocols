Robert Peake is a British-American poet living near London. His full-length collections include *Cyclone* (Nine Arches Press) and *The Knowledge*. His poems have appeared in *North American Review*, *Poetry International*, *Magma*, *Acumen*, and *The North*, with commendations from the James Hearst Poetry Prize, the Indiana Review Poetry Prize, and three Pushcart Prize nominations.

Between 2013 and 2018 he founded and ran Transatlantic Poetry, a global online poetry reading series in partnership with The Poetry Society, Rattle, the Scottish Poetry Library, Kundiman, and many others. The full archive is at https://www.robertpeake.com/transatlantic-poetry, on YouTube, and on the Internet Archive.

From 2016 to 2019 he built and ran Poet Tips, a recommendation website that collected over 75,000 recommendations for more than 6,500 poets, with institutional support from the Scottish Poetry Library. The full dataset is published in open GraphML format on the Internet Archive for research use: https://www.robertpeake.com/poet-tips-website.

His film-poems have been screened at poetry-film festivals throughout the US, UK, and Europe. Archive: https://www.robertpeake.com/poetry/film-poems.

In July 2025 he delivered "The Infinite Game of Poetry: Protocols for Living, Listening, and Transcending the Rules" — a public talk that formalises poetic practice as a recursive rule-learning algorithm of the form P(r,n) = B(B(L(rₙ₋₁)) → rₙ), drawing on Marvin Bell's "Learn the rules, break the rules, make up new rules, break the new rules", a close reading of Marianne Moore's "The Fish" as protocol embodied in syllabic form, and AI-generated poems as negative exemplars. The Prufrock Protocol extends the same formal lens from solitary practice into a multi-participant, cryptographically signed setting. Slides and handouts: https://www.robertpeake.com/files/2025/07/poetry-protocols-handouts.pdf.

He is an active member of the Formal Protocol Theory Special Interest Group (Roam, Discord, *Protocolized* on Substack, the Protocol Institute on YouTube). He has also assembled a comparative review of seven prior poetry-on-blockchain projects — Po.et, Mirror, Etherpoems, theVERSEverse, POEM, TECHNELEGY, and *Cord* — to ground The Prufrock Protocol in what has already been tried.

Personal site: https://www.robertpeake.com
