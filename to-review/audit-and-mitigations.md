## Mitigations — ranked by leverage

### 1. Write a separate pitch document

The lecture is appendix-grade proof-of-thinking; the pitch is its own artefact. Recommended structure (1–2 pages or 6–8 slides): thesis sentence • why-now • lineage paragraph • v1 deliverable • timeline • ask • who.

### 2. Name the v1 artefact

Three options considered:

- **(a) Printed monograph / chapbook** — *"Protocols of Poetry"* — codifies the P_M/P_NC/P_S taxonomy, the algorithm, and the first-/second-order distinction with worked examples. Durable object, sits naturally beside Rosetta as a Long Now reference.
- **(b) Public-domain protocol-poem corpus** — versioned, annotated, links each form (sonnet, ghazal, cynghanedd) to its rules. Sits naturally beside Long Bets as a formalized cultural artefact.
- **(c) Long Now seminar + companion essay** — lower commitment, lower durability. Good as warm-up, not as headline.

**Recommendation: (a) as v1, (b) as v2.** (a) is shippable in agentic-time + Robert's hours and gives Long Now the durable object they fund.

### 3. Add a Long Now lineage paragraph

Situate Prufrock Protocol against:

- **Eno's generative rule-systems** — poetry as engineered emergence
- **The Rosetta Disk** — poems as compression algorithms (already on slide 5 of the deck)
- **Long Bets** — formal speech acts with rules and consequences

Three sentences, written explicitly into the pitch.

### 4. Sharpen the thesis to one sentence

Working draft:

> Poetry is the oldest engineered protocol for transmitting compressed cultural state across long time horizons; the algorithm — learn the rules, break the rules, make new rules — is what makes the transmission durable, not what corrupts it.

Long Now hears "long time horizons + transmission." Protocols Institute hears "engineered protocol + recursion." Same sentence.

### 5. Reframe "Prufrock" so the irony pays

Two options:

- **Lean in** — open the pitch by acknowledging the joke. *"Prufrock could not commit. The protocol bears his name because hesitation — compromise plus conflict — is the engine of every poem worth keeping."* Turns the title from a liability into a thesis. The deck already names *tension = compromise + conflict* as the fundamental — Prufrock is the human face of that tension.
- **Step back** — keep "Prufrock Protocol" as project codename / chapter title, lead the pitch with "The Infinite Game of Poetry."

**Recommendation: lean in.**

### 6. Confirm Protocols Institute as named co-pitcher

Before anything goes out. Until signed, refer to them as "in conversation with" rather than "in collaboration with."

### 7. Phase the ask

- **v1** = printed monograph + Long Now seminar (6 months)
- **v2** = corpus (12+ months)

Write into the pitch so the foundation isn't being asked to commit to v2 sight unseen.

### 8. PD-anchor the demonstrations

McGonagall, Whitman, Eliot's *Prufrock*, GPT-generated examples — these carry the argument in any pitch document or printed artefact. Brecht / Bell / Heaney get brief fair-use quotation; permissions become a publication-stage problem, not a pitch-stage problem.

### 9. One-line definition of done in the pitch

> Done = monograph published with [imprint] and seminar delivered at Long Now by [date].
